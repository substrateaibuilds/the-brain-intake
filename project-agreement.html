The Brain.

Read this in full before signing. This document defines what you're buying, what I'm building, and what happens if anything goes sideways. If anything is unclear, reply to the email this came from before you sign.

01

What you're getting

I will build a Brain — a structured Obsidian knowledge vault — from your coaching archive. The Brain is built to the seven-element architecture: Distinctions, Evolution, Domains, Voice, Confidence, Conflict, and Corrections.

Specifically, I will:

  1. Process the archive you share with me — coaching call recordings, course modules, podcast appearances, written materials, and any other source content delivered via the shared folder we set up on your kickoff call.
  2. Transcribe any audio or video files that don't already have transcripts.
  3. Build structured knowledge pages for every major concept, protocol, and framework in your methodology.
  4. Cross-link all seven structural elements throughout the vault.
  5. Deliver the completed vault as an Obsidian folder you own outright.

What this engagement does not include:

The AI bot, assistant, or product that runs on top of the Brain — that's a separate engagement, if you choose to continue. The Brain is the data foundation. The product built on it is its own project.

Also excluded: website or marketing materials, anything requiring content you don't provide, and legal review of your existing client consent agreements.

02

Deliverable

A completed Obsidian vault containing:

  • Source pages — one structured page per coaching call, course module, or major recorded session in your archive, with title, date, domain tags, and key concepts extracted
  • Concept pages — one page per distinct concept in your methodology, with the seven structural elements populated
  • Domain indexes — navigable indexes for each domain area you teach
  • Corrections registry — a dated log of every tracked position shift in your vault
  • Voice blocks — verbatim language anchors throughout, sourced from recordings

The vault is delivered as a folder you open in Obsidian on your own machine. No subscription. No platform dependency. Yours forever, portable to any AI product you ever build on top of it.

03

04

Your commitments

The build cannot proceed without the following from you:

  • File delivery. Shared folder populated with organized files within 7 days of your kickoff call.
  • Response time. You will respond to clarifying questions within the turnaround time you committed to in Section 8 of the intake form. If I ask a question and don't hear back within your committed timeframe, the timeline extends by the number of days I waited. I won't chase.
  • Review pass. When I notify you that the vault is ready for review, you will complete it within 14 days. If 14 days passes without a response, the vault is considered accepted and delivered.
  • Obsidian installed. You are responsible for having Obsidian installed before the delivery call. It is free at obsidian.md.

06

Revision scope

After delivery, you have a 14-day correction window. If you find a factual error — something the vault states that directly contradicts what you teach — I will correct it at no charge.

The correction window covers factual accuracy. It does not cover scope expansion.

  • Factual error (covered): "This page says I recommend X but I have never taught X."
  • Scope expansion (not covered): "This page is missing a concept I didn't provide in my archive."

After the 14-day correction window, any additional work is scoped and priced as a separate engagement.

The review pass — where you spot-check 15–20 representative pages and approve the vault before delivery — is the quality gate. Approval of the review pass is acceptance of the deliverable.

07

Data handling

You own the Brain. The vault I deliver is built entirely from your content, your methodology, and your words. I claim no ownership over it. You may use it, modify it, sell products built on it, license it to others, or do anything else you want with it.

What I keep: The process and methodology I use to build Brains. The seven-element architecture is my framework, not yours — but your content applied to it is entirely yours.

Your source files: All source files you share with me are kept confidential, used only for building your Brain, and permanently deleted from my systems within 30 days of delivery.

Client data: Any recordings containing client conversations will be processed with client names anonymized throughout the vault. Real client names never appear in finished vault pages, regardless of what appears in the source recordings.

08

What "delivered" means

Delivery is complete when:

  1. The vault has been transferred to you as a folder you can open in Obsidian, AND
  2. You have completed or waived the review pass.

After these two conditions are met, the engagement is complete. Any further work — additional processing, new content, product builds — is a separate engagement.
